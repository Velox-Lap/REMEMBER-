# PROJECT 29.12 — Creative Interaction Roadmap

The visual language remains nature-led, cinematic, editorial, and quiet. These are original adaptations of broad interaction patterns commonly explored in Figma Community and Figma Sites; they are not claims of copying the nine linked Riffs, whose individual content was not accessible in this session.

## Guardrails

- No backend, accounts, tracking, or external runtime dependency.
- No autoplay audio.
- Touch and keyboard remain first-class; hover is enhancement only.
- Reduced-motion preferences disable ambient marquee and decorative sparkle loops.
- Personal photos, names, dates, and memories remain explicit placeholders until supplied.
- Avoid adding interactions that compete with the letter or final reveal.
